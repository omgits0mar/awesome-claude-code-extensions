# Community code of conduct

Be constructive, specific, and respectful.

- Discuss projects and evidence, not people.
- Disclose affiliations when submitting your own project or a sponsored resource.
- Do not harass maintainers, contributors, or reporters.
- Do not publish secrets, personal data, or exploit details in public issues.
- Do not manipulate placement with fake stars, copied projects, misleading claims, or paid submissions.
- Accept corrections when evidence changes.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, promotional, unsafe, or off-topic contributions and may restrict repeat offenders. Good-faith disagreement about inclusion, licensing, or risk is welcome when supported by concrete evidence.
